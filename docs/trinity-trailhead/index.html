How to choose a Trinity Trails starting point

The right trailhead matters more than an ambitious mileage goal.

By DFW Outside · Research-based guide · September 30, 2026

Work backward from your needs

The Trinity Trails official map helps you explore the network and its access points. Start with what your group needs: convenient parking, an appropriate surface, or a short distance. Then identify a trailhead that fits instead of navigating to the river in general.

Verify the actual section

Check current notices and map information for the route you intend to use. Do not infer a section’s accessibility or bicycle suitability from the overall network description. If a facility is essential, confirm it at the selected location. Have a shorter alternative in mind if the starting point is busy or conditions change.

Keep a useful record

After the outing, note where you parked and where you turned around. Those details make the next visit easier than a generic recommendation to walk the Trinity. You can build a personal collection of reliable short routes over time, adding distance only when the group wants it.
